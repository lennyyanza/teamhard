01 / The mission

Two environments.
One platform.

Fly to a site. Enter the water. Work below the surface. Return to the air.

Our senior design project explores a vehicle built for that complete mission, with speed, stability, and responsive control at its core.

The problem we’re working on ↗

02 / Engineering direction

Designed around the transition.

01

Shared propulsion

A single motor selects an aerial or underwater drive path through its direction of rotation.

02

Responsive control

PX4 attitude control and FPGA-based onboard inference bring the two operating modes together.

03

Simulation first

Validate the vehicle model before committing to a physical build, then move from bench to pool to open water.
